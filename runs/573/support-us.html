Built to be trusted, not just used

  • QR-only payments through your own UPI app — Gaming Horizon never asks for card or bank details.
  • No card or bank details are ever collected — only the amount and the UPI transaction reference you submit afterwards, for manual verification.
  • Every payment is checked by hand against the project’s own UPI/bank statement before a badge is granted — nothing is auto-approved.
  • No ads, no data-selling. Supporter contributions are the intended funding model.
